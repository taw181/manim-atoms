# Data shipped with aionanim

Read through `aionanim.resources.data_path(...)`.

| Path | What | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dai_fringes/{lln,hln}.csv` | Shot-by-shot excitation fractions of the AION prototype differential atom interferometer, low and high laser noise | C. Baynham et al., arXiv:2504.09158; data at doi:10.5281/zenodo.19592552, extracted by `data_scripts/process_dai_fringes.py` | CC BY 4.0 |
| `dai_fringes/adev.csv` | Allan deviation of the DAI differential phase for both runs, with the Monte Carlo SQL band (Fig. 4c) | Same paper and record: the `allan_deviation` sheet of `figures/Figure_4_data.xlsx`, extracted by `data_scripts/process_dai_adev.py` | CC BY 4.0 |
| `dai_fringes/sigma.csv` | Fig. 4c inset: scatter of the mean differential phase over each whole run, sigma_<dphi>, for both runs and the standard quantum limit | Same paper and record: `intermediate_data/sigma_delta_phi_*.npy` and the shot counts, reduced as `figures/Figure 4.ipynb` does, by `data_scripts/process_dai_adev.py` | CC BY 4.0 |
| `dai_fringes/signals.csv` | Likelihood of a fitted sinusoid against trial frequency for seven phases imprinted by a light shift (0.1–100 mHz), over each drive's periodogram (Fig. 5a) | Same paper and record: `signal_fitting_results_by_frequency.csv` and `precomputed_true_signals/`, reduced as `figures/Figure 5a.ipynb` does by `data_scripts/process_dai_signals.py` | CC BY 4.0 |
| `gw_sensitivity/*.csv` | Characteristic-strain sensitivity curves of LIGO, LISA, ET, AION-10, AION-100, AION-km, AEDGE, AEDGE+ | Digitised from `GW_exclusion_plot.svg` (see each CSV's header for the exact path and calibration); `aion_10.csv` and `aion_100.csv` by `data_scripts/digitise_aion_gw.py`, whose `--check` reproduces `aion_km.csv` to 0.001 dex | Belongs to the owner of the source figure: check before redistributing |
| `hifais_loading/{curves,fits}.csv` | 3D MOT atom number against loading time at four oven temperatures (400–465 °C), mean and SEM, and the fit of dN/dt = R − LN to each below 0.9e9 atoms (Fig. 5 inset) | T. Walker et al., AVS Quantum Sci. 8, 033201 (2026), doi:10.1116/5.0338570: the paper repo's `data/loading_data/`, refitted as its `scripts/mot3d/loading_rate.py` does by `data_scripts/process_hifais_loading.py` | Ours |
| `images/hifais_chamber_{top,side}.png` | CAD renders of the HiFAIS vacuum system from above and from the side, unlabelled, page keyed out (Fig. 1c,d) | Same paper: the raster images inside `figures/Chamber_InkS_4pane.pdf`, pulled out by `data_scripts/process_hifais_loading.py` | Ours |
| `images/ligo20160211a.jpg` | GW150914 as observed by LIGO Hanford and Livingston | LIGO press release, 11 Feb 2016 | Credit Caltech/MIT/LIGO Laboratory |
| `uldm_sensitivity/*.csv` | Projected sensitivity of AION-10, AION-100 (initial and goal), AION-km and AEDGE to scalar ULDM coupled to photons, \|d_e\| against m_phi (solid curves, f >= 0.3 Hz), and the lower edge of the region already excluded (torsion balances, MICROSCOPE, atomic clocks) | L. Badurina et al., JCAP 05 (2020) 011, arXiv:1911.11755, Fig. 3 middle panel: read from the vector paths of the journal PDF by `data_scripts/digitise_uldm_sensitivity.py` (calibration in each CSV's header). The CSVs hold only what the paper draws; `aionanim.physics.uldm_sensitivity.load_uldm_sensitivity` carries the AION curves on below 0.3 Hz with the paper's own model (eqs. 3.5-3.7, Table 1), which reproduces every digitised curve to a constant 0.20 dex, scaled onto each at its low-mass end | Belongs to the authors / JCAP: check before redistributing |
| `videos/midway_imaging_b059528_slow.mp4` | Side-on imaging of the Sr cloud from the blue MOT through loading the upper then the lower dipole trap; 48 snapshots, t = 10–599 ms, each held for 4 frames at 10 fps | AION prototype imaging, supplied by T. Walker (the `b059528` in the name is the commit that made it) | Unpublished: check with the owner before redistributing |
